07 — THE HOUSE RULES

Whole spices, full-creammilk and Assam leaf —boiled up three timesand poured from a height.It takes eleven minutes.We have stoppedapologising.

01

Nothing from a tin.

Ginger, cardamom, clove, cinnamon and pepper are bruised by hand at 5:40 every morning. If you can't smell it from the street, we start again.

02

Three boils. No shortcuts.

Climb to the rim, drop the flame, climb again. The third rise is where the tea turns from brown to that colour.

03

Poured, not ladled.

A metre of air between pan and glass. It cools the chai one degree and puts the froth on top.
